Professional Profile

About Dr. Kiruthiga

Associate Professor of Pharmaceutical Chemistry with over 18.5 years of teaching experience and a sustained, fast-growing research record in computer-aided drug design and medicinal chemistry.

Research core centers on the design, synthesis and computational-to-experimental evaluation of flavonoid, chromone, pyrazoline and heterocyclic scaffolds against diabetes, inflammation, infection and cancer targets, with current work extending into HDAC-inhibitor design, machine-learning-assisted drug discovery, and marine bioactive compounds.

Author of 41 peer-reviewed publications (predominantly Scopus/Web of Science/PubMed-indexed, including 18 in Q1/Q2 journals since 2025), 6 patents, 3 authored/edited books and 4 book chapters, alongside a strong record of UG/PG mentorship, NBA/NAAC accreditation leadership, and peer review for Elsevier, Springer and Bentham Science journals.
