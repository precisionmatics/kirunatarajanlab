About Dr. Kiruthiga

Associate Professor with 18.5 years of teaching and research, pioneering the translation of computational insight into medicinal molecules — flavonoids, heterocycles, and HDAC inhibitors designed in silico, validated in vitro.

NBA Criterion 1 Coordinator
Nominated PCI Inspector
Ph.D. Advisory Committee Member, VMRF
Peer Reviewer — Elsevier, Springer, Bentham Science

Vision

Advance drug discovery through computational and medicinal chemistry; build institutional research strength through funded projects and scholarly output.

Mission

  • Secure extramural grants (DST, ICMR, AICTE, BIRAC, TNSCST)
  • Mentor UG/PG/PhD scholars toward publication-ready research
  • Elevate institutional accreditation through outcome-based education
Expertise

Core Research Areas

Computer-Aided Drug Design

Molecular docking, MD simulation, virtual screening, QSAR modelling and computational toxicology using Maestro, AutoDock & BIOVIA Discovery Studio.

Molecular DockingMD SimulationQSAR

Medicinal Chemistry

Design and synthesis of flavonoid, chromone, pyrazoline and heterocyclic scaffolds with full spectroscopic characterization.

FlavonoidsChromonesPyrazolines

Drug Discovery

In-silico, in-vitro and in-vivo pharmacological screening for anti-diabetic, anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial and anticancer targets.

Anti-diabeticAnticancerAnti-inflammatory

HDAC Inhibitor Design

Design and synthesis of HDAC inhibitors for apoptosis induction in cervical cancer cells, including piperidine hydroxamate scaffolds.

AI & Machine Learning

Machine learning and explainable AI in cheminformatics; Python-based tools for molecular fingerprint visualization and prediction.
