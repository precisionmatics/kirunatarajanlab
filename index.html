Professional Profile

About Dr. Kiruthiga

Associate Professor of Pharmaceutical Chemistry with over 18.5 years of teaching experience and a sustained, fast-growing research record in computer-aided drug design and medicinal chemistry.

Research core centers on the design, synthesis and computational-to-experimental evaluation of flavonoid, chromone, pyrazoline and heterocyclic scaffolds against diabetes, inflammation, infection and cancer targets, with current work extending into HDAC-inhibitor design, machine-learning-assisted drug discovery, and marine bioactive compounds.

Author of 41 peer-reviewed publications (predominantly Scopus/Web of Science/PubMed-indexed, including 18 in Q1/Q2 journals since 2025), 6 patents, 3 authored/edited books and 4 book chapters.

NBA Criterion 1 Coordinator
Nominated PCI Inspector
Ph.D. Advisory Committee Member, VMRF
Reviewer: Elsevier, Springer, Bentham Science

Vision

To advance drug discovery through computational and medicinal chemistry, and to build institutional research strength through funded research, scholarly output, and academic excellence.

Mission

  • Secure extramural grants (DST, ICMR, AICTE, BIRAC, TNSCST)
  • Mentor UG/PG/PhD scholars toward publication-ready, patentable research
  • Elevate institutional accreditation standing through outcome-based education
